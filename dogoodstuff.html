Our mission

Help people do good stuff — for themselves and for others.

We believe technology has a problem. Most of it is designed to consume your attention, keeping you on a device rather than in the world.

We want to build for a different outcome. Tools that serve a mission of public good — and then get out of the way. Tools that help you do good things for yourself, for the people around you, and for your community.

We want you to exist in the real, physical world. Not a virtual one. We want you to see real, local people, places and things. Not feeds always meant to take you far away.

We want to use technology to deepen your observation, desire to act, and feeling of meaning. And then we want you to put the phone away and live your life.
